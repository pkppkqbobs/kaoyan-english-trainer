第 1 题

A railway operator provides step-free route maps on its website, but the maps are revised only twice a year. When a lift broke last spring, wheelchair users began sharing their own route map. It was updated as soon as another lift failed and was soon consulted more often than the official guide.

That is no reason for the operator to congratulate itself on the availability of information. When people come to rely on a guide that its staff neither check nor maintain, we ought to ask why the service for which the operator is responsible has become so unreliable.

How does the author view the popularity of the unofficial map?

we ought to ask why the service for which the operator is responsible has become so unreliable

走红本身不是作者赞许的理由，而是现行服务有问题的证据。

文中明确否定运营方值得庆贺；没有说故障减少，也不能由乘客依赖一张地图推断他们讨厌所有地图。

第 2 题

A city tool library used to charge members every time they borrowed a drill or a saw. Under a new agreement, residents can borrow the tools at no charge. The city now pays the library a fixed monthly fee, and it pays a separate charge whenever a borrowed tool needs a repair.

Workshop managers welcome the change because inexperienced users are no longer put off by a fee at the counter. The finance office is less sure that the city will spend less over the year: the repairs have yet to be tallied.

What do the new borrowing terms allow?

Under a new agreement, residents can borrow the tools at no charge. The city now pays the library a fixed monthly fee

居民不付借用费，不等于服务本身无成本，也不等于市政府少花钱。

市政府仍承担维修费用；图书馆仍收市政府月费；维修费用尚未汇总，经理不能据此预测全年成本。

第 3 题

In several towns, craft fairs are organised by volunteers who spend months arranging tables, checking fire exits and helping stallholders set up. The volunteers are not paid. Many take part because the fairs give them a chance to be known for work they take pride in.

The largest financial return does not go to the people who put the fairs together. It goes to the company that holds the exclusive advertising rights. The better attended the volunteer-run events become, the more that company can charge for access to the crowds.

Which feature of the fairs does the passage emphasise?

The largest financial return does not go to the people who put the fairs together. It goes to the company that holds the exclusive advertising rights.

组织者与利润获得者不是同一方，中心是收益分配而非工作是否胜过声望。

没说组织者为展位付费或志愿者赚门票钱；希望被认可的是志愿者，不是广告主。

第 4 题

A town licenses traders to use its main square on Saturdays. The licensing rules have not been struck down by a court, but few traders believe those rules are still a fair way to allocate space. A town review recommends keeping the Saturday market while changing how stalls are assigned. At present, traders apply for a space at the town hall each week. The review suggests that applications should be collected in advance so that traders who work outside the town can also take part.

What does the passage say about the licensing rules?

The licensing rules have not been struck down by a court, but few traders believe those rules are still a fair way to allocate space.

仍然有效与失去使用者认同可以同时成立。

法院没有宣布违法；审核建议保留市场，也没有主张全面废除许可。
